About Us

BBK Gardens is a modern and luxurious event venue located in Nyanama. We provide elegant gardens, accommodation, music, bar services, delicious meals, ample parking, and a relaxing environment for all guests.

Whether it is a wedding reception, birthday party, Kwanjula, graduation party, or family gathering, BBK Gardens offers the perfect atmosphere.
